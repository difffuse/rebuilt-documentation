**Retopo · UV · Bake** — turn complex models into clean production assets.

Rebuilt brings retopology, UV creation and texture baking into one Blender workflow. Start with a scan, sculpt or detailed mesh, then build a lighter model with its surface detail transferred to textures.

<DocFigure name="overview" />

## Choose your workflow

**Auto** runs the complete process from a few settings. Choose your model type, geometry budget and texture resolution, then click **Rebuild Asset**.

**Manual** lets you review each step: **Retopologise → Seams → Unwrap → Bake Maps**. Use it to edit seams, keep an existing UV layout or refine one stage without starting over.

Once the model is complete, export it to **Unity**, **Unreal** or **Godot**, or keep working with it in Blender.
